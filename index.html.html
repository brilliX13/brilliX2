How it works

Three simple rules make the experience better.

The book's opening guidance is intentionally simple: choose one activity, move the phone away, and start before you feel completely ready.

01
Pick one, not ten.

Choose one activity that matches the time and energy you have now.

02
Park the phone out of reach.

Put it in a drawer, basket, or another room - out of sight is easier.

03
Start before you feel ready.

The first minutes are the hardest; the activity can carry you forward.
